Alert Email

Receive an email when a tracked person appears in the news.

How it works

  1. Add people you want to track (IAS Officers, STU bodies, Ministers…)
  2. Set your email above to receive alerts
  3. The pipeline automatically scrapes government news sites every hour
  4. When a match is found, it stores the article and emails you

Verify it's working

No email yet? That's normal if no tracked person appeared in recent news. You can:

  • Click Run Now in the top bar and check the log output
  • Open activity_tracker.db in DB Browser for SQLite to inspect the articles table
  • Rows with has_keyword_match = 1 = a match was found
  • Rows with email_sent = 1 = alert was emailed
